# Walkthrough — Slice 5.6: Lifecycle (cancel, suspend, reactivate, delete)

Purpose: drive the real app once, by hand, before building 5.7 on top of it.
About 30 minutes, plus a 5-minute wait in Part 3. Record pass / fail as you go.

**Two throwaway tenants are used, and you can name them anything obviously
fake.** Nothing here touches Acme Test Prospect or your other real test tenants.
Part 2 (Stripe) is optional and best done with me.

## Before you start

- All four processes must be running: API (8000), web (3000), Celery worker,
  Celery beat. Beat is what suspends tenants on schedule. I started them; tell
  me if any stops.
- Open **http://localhost:3000/login** (use `localhost`, not `127.0.0.1`), sign
  in as the founder, then go to **http://localhost:3000/admin**.
- The top bar shows: Attention · Intakes · Tenants · Lifecycle · Outbox.

---

## Part 1 — Create two throwaway tenants (5 min)

1. Top bar → **Tenants** → **New tenant** (or go to `/admin/tenants/new`).
2. Fill in: **Company name** `Acme Test Lifecycle A`; **Owner email**
   `lifecycle-a@example.test`; **Intake** `None`; **Primary currency** `USD`;
   **Timezone** e.g. `America/New_York`. Under **Deal terms** pick any option
   (e.g. waived, which needs a note).
3. Submit.
   - **Expect:** you land on the new tenant's page. Status **active**. No
     Stripe subscription (it has not been through go-live).
4. Repeat for `Acme Test Lifecycle B` / `lifecycle-b@example.test`.
   - **Expect:** both appear in the **Tenants** list.

## Part 2 — Cancel form on tenant A (10 min)

On tenant A's page, scroll to the **Lifecycle** card and click **Open
Lifecycle →**. (URL: `/admin/tenants/<id>/lifecycle`.)

**Expect on arrival:** heading "Lifecycle — Acme Test Lifecycle A"; a grey box
with Status `active` and Stripe subscription `none`; a **Cancel tenant** form.

| # | Do this | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Leave **Reason** = *Customer requested* | An amber box appears: **Effective date** = right now, **Rule:** "no subscription yet: immediate". (No warning line, because no subscription is a known case.) |
| 6 | Change **Reason** to *Non-payment* | Amber box updates: date = now, **Rule:** "the first past-due notice (Net terms are the only grace period, D-125)", plus the warning line "No billing record to compute this from yet — double-check before confirming." |
| 7 | Change **Reason** to *For cause* | A **Reason for cause (at least 20 characters)** box appears. Rule: "immediate (for cause)". The **Cancel this tenant…** button is **greyed out**. |
| 8 | Type `too short` in the box | Button stays greyed out. |
| 9 | Type 20+ characters, e.g. `Testing the for cause path on a fake tenant` | Button becomes clickable. |
| 10 | Set **Push the effective date later** to a moment in the **past** (e.g. yesterday), click **Cancel this tenant…** then **Confirm cancellation** | A red error box titled **"The effective date can only move later"** (code LIFE-003). Nothing changes; status stays `active`. |
| 11 | Clear that date field. Do not cancel yet. | — |

**Now cancel tenant A properly, with *Customer requested*** (pick it again,
leave the date blank):

| # | Do this | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | **Cancel this tenant…** | A red paragraph: "Everything keeps working until the effective date above. After that, intake stops and billing is cancelled, but the tenant can still sign in and export until the deletion date." with **Confirm cancellation** and **Back** buttons. **Back** returns you to the form. |
| 13 | **Confirm cancellation** | Green message "Cancellation scheduled, effective <today's date>." The cancel form disappears. |

**If you pushed the effective date to a later day** (as happened in the first run), the tenant
correctly stays **cancelling** and everything keeps working for it. It only moves to the
wind-down queue once that date passes. That is the intended behaviour, not a fault.

Because tenant A has no subscription, its effective date is *now*, so the
sweep in Part 3 will suspend it on its next pass (within ~5 minutes).

## Part 3 — Suspend happens on its own (5–10 min)

1. Top bar → **Outbox**.
   - **Expect:** an email to `lifecycle-a@example.test` with subject
     "DocFlow: your account for Acme Test Lifecycle A is scheduled to end",
     showing an **Effective date** and a **Deletion date** about 30 days later.
     (Emails are held in the Outbox because no email provider is configured;
     that is expected, not a fault.)
2. Wait up to 5 minutes (beat runs the sweep every 5 minutes), then reload
   tenant A's Lifecycle page.
   - **Expect:** Status **pending_deletion** (suspended and pending-deletion
     happen in one step by design, D-123). **Cancellation reason**, **Effective
     date**, and **Deletion date** (~30 days out) are now shown.
   - **Expect:** the **Reactivate** panel (green) replaces the cancel form.
   - **Expect in Outbox:** a second email, a suspension notice.
3. Top bar → **Lifecycle** (`/admin/lifecycle`).
   - **Expect:** under **Wind-down queue**, "Acme Test Lifecycle A",
     "customer_requested · deletes <date>", and "30 days left" (or 29).
   - **Expect:** **Ready to delete** says "Nothing is ready to delete right now."
   - Nothing was deleted automatically.
4. Top bar → **Attention** (`/admin`).
   - **Expect:** an alert that the tenant entered pending deletion.

## Part 4 — Reactivate (3 min)

1. From Wind-down queue click **Acme Test Lifecycle A** → you are on its
   Lifecycle page. Click **Reactivate**.
   - **Expect:** green message "Reactivated."; Status **active**; the cancel
     form is back; the tenant's onboarding status is unchanged; nothing lost.
   - **Expect in Outbox:** a reactivation email.
   - **Judge:** was Reactivate as easy to find and click as Cancel? (The spec
     requires it to be.)
2. Top bar → **Lifecycle**: tenant A is no longer in the wind-down queue.

## Part 5 — For cause + delete on tenant B (10 min)

1. Open tenant B → **Open Lifecycle →**. Reason **For cause**, note of 20+
   characters, **Cancel this tenant…** → **Confirm cancellation**.
   - **Expect:** "Cancellation scheduled, effective <today>."
2. Wait for the sweep (up to 5 min). **Expect:** B appears in the Wind-down
   queue.
3. **Tell me when B is in the wind-down queue.** The delete button only appears
   once the deletion window has elapsed (30 days), so I will move B's deletion
   date into the past directly in the database for the test, as the automated
   tests do. Tell me the tenant name; I'll do it and say when it's done.
4. Reload **Lifecycle**.
   - **Expect:** B is now under **Ready to delete** (red box) with a
     **Delete…** button.
5. Click **Delete…**.
   - **Expect:** a warning and two fields. The **Permanently delete** button is
     greyed out.
6. Type the name wrongly (e.g. `acme test lifecycle b`, wrong capitals) and a
   reason of 10+ characters.
   - **Expect:** button still greyed out (the name must match exactly).
7. Type the exact name `Acme Test Lifecycle B` and a reason like `walkthrough
   test`. Click **Permanently delete**.
   - **Expect:** B disappears from Ready to delete. **Do not expect it to vanish
     from history:** its tenant row and lifecycle events are kept by design
     (D-123); only business data and files are removed.

## Part 6 — Access control (2 min)

*Fixed after the first run (D-127):* a signed-out visitor to `/admin/lifecycle` used to see a blank
page; it now shows the standard **404 - This page could not be found**.

1. Open a private/incognito window (so you are signed out) and go to
   `http://localhost:3000/admin/lifecycle`.
   - **Expect:** you are not shown the Console; you are sent to login or shown
     nothing about it. It must never look like an "access denied" page that
     confirms `/admin` exists.
2. Ask me to run the API check for a signed-in tenant user (not the founder).
   **Expect** a 404, not a 403. (This is already an automated test; this is a
   second look.)
