# Walkthrough — Slice 5.1: Console foundations (onboarding Steps 1–3)

Purpose: start a new customer the way you will for real. Record the prospect,
stage their files, create the tenant, and invite its owner. Along the way,
check the pieces slice 5.1 laid down: the tiers table, intake staging, the
email outbox, founder alerts and invites. About 20 minutes. No model calls.

**This starts a new test tenant, Acme Test UAT Supply**, which 5.2 and 5.3
take through to go-live. It repeats Phase 5's first exit criterion (all nine
onboarding steps from the Console, ending with a live tenant whose owner
signs in by invite) on today's code.

## Before you start

- All four processes running. Test files made (see [README](README.md)).
- Sign in as yourself.
- The owner's email will be `uat-owner@example.test`. Nothing is really
  emailed (no provider yet), so any address works; you'll copy the invite link
  from the Outbox.

---

## Part 1 — Step 1: Intake staging (6 min)

1. Top bar → **Intakes**. **Expect:** "Record a prospect, then upload the
   files they emailed you…", a short form, and the list of earlier intakes.
2. Fill in **Prospect company** `Acme Test UAT Supply`, **Contact email**
   `uat-owner@example.test`, **Notes** `walkthrough`. **Start intake**.
   - **Expect:** you're on the intake's page: its name, the email, the time
     received, and two numbered boxes: **1. Add the prospect's files** and
     **2. Create the tenant**.
3. **Choose files…** and pick:
   - `DocFlow\catalog-samples\1-clean-catalog.xlsx`
   - `DocFlow\walkthrough-files\onboarding\customer-list.csv`
   - five sample orders from `walkthrough-files\good`: `01-…pdf`, `02-…pdf`,
     `04-…xlsx`, `15-…doc`, `19-…msg`
   - **Expect:** **Uploaded (7)**, each with its detected type, size, and the
     first 12 characters of its SHA-256.
4. Now **Choose files…** → `walkthrough-files\hostile\h01-archive-with-a-po.zip`.
   - **Expect:** a red box titled **h01-archive-with-a-po.zip: That file isn't
     one DocFlow accepts** (CON-007). Staging uses the same checks as customer
     uploads; a prospect's file is untrusted too. It's not added to the list.
5. **Intakes** again. **Expect** your intake in the list: 7 files, Tenant **Not
   created yet**.

## Part 2 — Step 2: Create the tenant (5 min)

1. Open the intake → **Create the tenant from this intake →**.
   - **Expect:** "Create a tenant", with **Company name** and **Owner email**
     already filled from the intake, and **Intake** set to it.
2. **Plan** dropdown. **Expect:** Starter, Growth and Scale, each with its
   price and allowance. These come from the tiers table, not from code
   (Section 10: no price typed into code). Compare them with
   `docflow-pricing.docx`.
3. Set the deal. You'll change it in 5.3, so any choice will do:
   - **Plan:** Starter. **Founding customer:** unticked.
   - **Setup fee:** Standard. **How the setup fee is billed:** On the first
     Stripe invoice.
4. Leave **Primary currency** `USD` and **Timezone** `America/New_York`.
   **Create tenant**.
   - **Expect:** the tenant's page. Under the name: "active · onboarding:
     tenant created". The overview shows:
     - **Plan:** Starter (v…), with price and allowance
     - **Owner:** `uat-owner@example.test · not invited yet`
     - **Intake address:** a long address with a random token, "· not live
       until go-live"
     - **Stripe customer:** a `cus_…` id
5. Back on the intake. **Expect:** "These files now belong to the tenant
   created from this intake", with a link. The upload box is gone.
6. **Stripe (test mode):** open the Stripe dashboard → Customers. **Expect** an
   **Acme Test UAT Supply** customer and no subscription. Nothing is billed
   until go-live.

## Part 3 — Step 3: Invite the owner (6 min)

1. On the tenant page, **Invite the owner** → **Send invite**.
   - **Expect:** "Invite created. No email provider is set up yet, so it is
     held in the outbox below — copy the link from there." The button now
     says **Send the invite again**, with "Last sent …".
2. Under **Emails to this tenant**, open **Your DocFlow account for Acme Test
   UAT Supply is ready** (marked **held — not sent**). Read it as the
   customer would. Copy its link.
3. Open a **private window** and paste the link.
   - **Expect:** "Choose your password", naming `uat-owner@example.test`.
4. Try a 5-character password. **Expect:** "That password is shorter than 10
   characters…". Try two that don't match. **Expect:** "The two passwords
   don't match, so nothing was saved…".
5. Set a real one (write it down; 5.3 and 5.8 use it). **Save password and
   continue**.
   - **Expect:** you're signed in as the owner, on **Purchase orders**, empty.
     The header shows **Acme Test UAT Supply**.
6. Paste the same link again in a fresh private window.
   - **Expect:** "This invite link has expired". Links work once. The page
     offers no way to sign up.
7. Back in the Console, reload the tenant page. **Expect:** Owner
   `· invited`.

## Part 4 — The Outbox and alerts (3 min)

1. Top bar → **Outbox**. **Expect** every email DocFlow has tried to send, for
   all tenants, newest first, each with its tenant's name. Yours is at the
   top.
2. Top bar → **Attention**. **Expect** "Needs your attention" and the open
   alerts, most severe first, each with its tenant, time and details. On 25
   Sept there were six (an abuse ceiling, a past-due subscription, some
   lifecycle alerts, an allowance reached).
3. **Acknowledge** one of the low-severity ones. **Expect** it leaves the
   list.
4. Section 7.9 says one alert, one row, two channels: each alert should also
   have a matching founder email in the **Outbox**. Look for one. Note it if
   the alerts and the emails don't match up.
