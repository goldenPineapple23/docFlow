# Walkthrough — Slice 5.8a–c: The customer's own screens

Purpose: see DocFlow as a customer sees it, as their admin and as a reviewer.
Header navigation, the upload page, the admin's dashboard, what each role
can reach, the Activity page, and the needs-review digest email. The Team
page (5.8d) has [its own walkthrough](5.8d-team.md). About 30 minutes,
including a 15-minute wait. Two cents of model calls.

**Only two roles are handed out** (your decision, 5.8a): the account's
**Admin** (the owner, its first user) and **Reviewer**.

## Before you start

- 5.3 done: **Acme Test UAT Supply** is live; you know its owner's password.
- **Admin view:** `uat-owner@example.test` (Acme Test UAT Supply).
- **Reviewer view:** `walkthrough@example.test` (a reviewer on Acme Test
  Distributor).
- Use your normal window for one and a private window for the other. Keep a
  third tab signed in as yourself on the Console **Outbox**.

## Part 2 — Upload (5 min)

As the **admin**:

1. **Upload** → **Choose files** → `walkthrough-files\good\06-po-BCH-UAT-06.txt`
   and `…\08-po-BCH-UAT-08.md`.
2. Before sending, remove `08` with its **×**. **Expect** only `06` left and
   **Upload 1 file**. Send it. Note the time. The digest in Part 5 counts from
   here.
3. **Expect:** "1 of 1 file accepted". There's no duplicate warning: BCH-UAT-06
   is new to *this* tenant, even though Acme Test Distributor has it.
4. **About 5 minutes later**, upload `08-po-BCH-UAT-08.md` on its own.
5. **Take a photo:** on a phone this opens the camera. It can't be tried
   against `localhost` from a phone; note it for the live site.

## Part 3 — The admin's dashboard (6 min)

As the **admin** → **Dashboard**.

1. **Orders by status:** a tile per status with a count. **Expect** Needs
   review **2** (Part 2's uploads, once read) and Approved **5** (the setup
   batch). Click a tile. **Expect** the Purchase orders list on that tab.
2. **This month:** **Arrived**, **Counted toward your plan** (**2**: today's
   uploads, not the setup batch), **Approved**, **Typical time to approve**.
3. **Needs review** list: the two new orders, oldest first.
4. **Recent activity:** the last 10 events. Your 5.3 work shows as **DocFlow
   support**.
5. **Nothing about money.** No AI cost, no price, no invoice. This is the
   account's view, not yours.

## Part 4 — Activity (5 min)

1. As the **admin** → **Activity**. **Expect** every event on the account,
   newest first. The top rows are the same as the dashboard's Recent activity.
2. **Filter chips:** **Everything**, then one per kind: Edited, Approved,
   Rejected, Reopened, Exported, Released from hold, Invited, Removed from
   team (only kinds that have happened may show). Pick **Approved**. **Expect**
   only approvals, and the total count changes. One filter at a time.
3. Click a row. **Expect** its order opens.
4. **No order contents in the trail:** an edit says how many fields changed,
   never what they became. Find your 5.3 edit (the date and currency on
   BCH-UAT-04). **Fail if** it shows the values.
5. As the **reviewer** → **Activity** on Acme Test Distributor. **Expect** a
   long trail from Phases 2–4. If it runs past 50 events there are page
   controls, and the total stays on screen.

## Part 5 — The needs-review digest (a 15-minute wait)

At most one email per account per 15 minutes, to its admin and reviewers,
counting orders that arrived. It never names a PO, a buyer or an amount
(Section 7.10).

1. About 15 minutes after the **first** upload in Part 2, open the Console
   **Outbox** (reload; beat checks every few minutes).
2. **Expect one** email to `uat-owner@example.test`: "DocFlow: 2 orders ready
   for review" or similar, covering **both** uploads, not one email each.
   Open it:
   - counts and a link to Purchase orders
   - **Fail if** it shows a PO number, a buyer name or an amount
3. **No re-nagging:** leave the orders unreviewed. Check the Outbox again 15+
   minutes later. **Expect** no second email about the same orders.
4. Approve both orders (as the admin), so the account ends clean.
