# Walkthrough — Slice 5.7: allowances and quarantine

Purpose: drive the two new screens once, by hand, before building on them. About
30 minutes. The automated tests, and a live run I did against the real stack
(email in, ceiling tripped, documents held, released through the real worker),
have passed. This is about how it reads and feels.

**Use a test tenant.** Examples below use **Bella's Coffee Haus**
(`testing@test.com`), which has a tier. Any tenant with a tier and an owner login
works. Parts 1 – 4 are the Console (you as the founder). Part 5 is the customer's
view and needs that tenant's owner login. Part 6 is optional.

## Before you start

- API, web, worker and beat must be running (I restarted them on the new code).
- Migration `0021` is applied (it is).
- Open **http://localhost:3000/login** and sign in as the founder → `/admin`.

## Set up the demo data

From the repo folder, in a terminal:

```
apps\api\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\seed_quarantine_demo.py --tenant-of testing@test.com --usage 85
```

**Expect:** it prints that it seeded 6 held documents and that this month now
counts **255 of 300 documents (Starter)**. (If Bella's tier isn't Starter the
numbers will differ; the 85% is what matters.) Each held document has a real,
tiny, fictional PO file, so releasing one costs about a cent of model time.

To undo everything at the end: same command with `--clean` instead of `--usage 85`.

---

## Part 1 — The Console screen (5 min)

1. Top bar → **Tenants** → click **Bella's Coffee Haus** → find the card
   **Held documents and intake address** → **Open held documents →**.

**Expect, top to bottom:**
- Heading "Held documents — Bella's Coffee Haus".
- A box: **This month** `255 / 300 documents (Starter)`; **Held now** `6`.
- A list of reasons with counts, each in plain English (for example
  "2 attachment_cap: This email had more attachments than DocFlow allows...").
- A table with **Received, Reason, Sender / subject, File, SPF / DKIM / DMARC,
  SHA-256**. The "Urgent PO" row from `ap@seed-spoof.example` should show
  `fail / none / fail`.
- Oldest first. **Release selected (0)** is greyed out.

## Part 2 — Release, in order (5 min)

1. Tick the two **attachment_cap** rows, click **Release selected (2)**.

**Expect:**
- Green message "Released 2. They are being processed now, oldest first."
- The two rows disappear from the list; **Held now** drops to 4 and **This month**
  goes up by 2 (released documents start counting only now).
- Within about a minute those two documents appear in the tenant's review queue
  (Tenants → Bella's → the review link) in **Needs review**, read from the PO files.

## Part 3 — Clear needs the name typed (3 min)

1. Tick the **auth_fail** row → **Clear selected (1)…**
2. Type `bella's coffee haus` (wrong capitals).
   - **Expect:** the **Clear** button stays greyed out.
3. Type the exact name and click **Clear**.
   - **Expect:** "Cleared 1. They are hidden, not destroyed, until the retention
     period ends." and the row disappears.

## Part 4 — Approved senders, and replacing the address (5 min)

1. **Approved senders (optional):** the box is **unticked** by default, and the
   text says mail from anyone not listed is **held, never rejected**.
2. Tick it, enter `buyer@example.com` on one line and `example.org` on the next,
   **Save**.
   - **Expect:** "Approved-sender list is on." Reload the page: it is still on,
     with both lines.
3. Try saving with the box ticked and the list **empty**.
   - **Expect:** an error titled **"That approved-sender list can't be saved"**
     explaining an empty list would hold every order. Then untick it and Save
     ("Approved-sender list is off.").
4. **Replace the intake address:** click **Replace address…**, accept the prompt.
   - **Expect:** "New address issued: orders+…@… The old one replies until <date
     30 days out>."
   - The message also says the owner has been emailed. Click **Outbox** in the black bar
     at the top of the Console (right of **Lifecycle**): the newest email is to the tenant's
     owner, "DocFlow: your order address for Bella's Coffee Haus has changed", and contains
     the new address. Click it to open it.

## Part 5 — The customer's view (10 min)

Open a **private window**, go to `http://localhost:3000/login`, sign in as the
tenant's owner.

1. **Purchase orders** page. **Expect** two notices under the header:
   - A blue **allowance banner**: "You've used 255 of 300 documents included in
     Starter this month. Documents continue to process normally." and a line
     saying Growth includes 1,000 documents per month.
   - An amber strip: "4 documents are being held" (or however many are left),
     listing each reason in plain English, with **Open Held for review →**.
   - Neither blocks the queue underneath.
2. Click **Held for review** (also in the top bar).
   - **Expect** a group per reason. The **unknown senders** group has an action
     line. The **abuse ceiling** group says "DocFlow reviews these itself, so
     there is nothing for you to do. It has already been alerted." and its
     checkbox is **disabled**.
3. Tick an unknown-sender document → **Release selected (1)**.
   - **Expect:** "Released 1 document. They are being processed now, oldest
     first." and it leaves the list.
4. Expand **Ignored mail**. It may be empty for this tenant. That is fine.
5. **The 100% banner:** back in the terminal run the setup command with
   `--usage 100`. Reload the customer's Purchase orders page.
   - **Expect:** the banner now reads **"You've reached your monthly allowance"**
     with "300 of 300", and still says documents continue to process normally.

**Judge:** would a real customer understand why documents are held, and know
whether there is anything for them to do? Is any wording confusing or alarming?

## Part 6 — Optional, best done together

These are proven by automated tests and my live run, but you can watch them:

  address is held, the sender gets a "received and held" reply, and you get an

- **The 80% / 100% emails and the founder "allowance reached" alert:** these fire
  when a *real* document arrives and crosses the line, so they can't be shown by
  seeding. Same offer: I'll send one.

  checkbox disabled.

## Clean up

```
apps\api\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\seed_quarantine_demo.py --tenant-of testing@test.com --clean
```

It removes exactly the seeded documents and emails and nothing else. Any
documents you released stay, because they became real documents.
