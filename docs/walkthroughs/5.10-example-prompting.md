# Walkthrough — Slice 5.10: approved-example prompting

Purpose: see the feature through your own eyes. Check the Console switch and its
safety catch, what a reviewer sees on an order read with examples, and that
a new order is read with examples when it arrives the normal way. About 15
minutes, with a few cents of real model calls.

Use **Acme Test Prospect**. On 25 Sept it was given 10 approved Bella's Coffee
House orders through the real pipeline (`scripts/seed_example_history.py`), and
example prompting was switched on there for the drive. Sign in as yourself at
**http://localhost:3000/login**. All four local servers must be running (API,
web, worker, beat).

## Part 1 — The Console page

1. Console → **Tenants** → **Acme Test Prospect** → **Example prompting →**.
2. Check:
   - It says **Currently on. 1 customer would get examples.**
   - The table lists **Bella's Coffee House** with **11** approved, **10** usable, **Yes**. One of the 11 was approved before 25 Sept, so DocFlow kept no text for it.
   - **Acme Test Cafe** shows **Not yet (9 more)**.
3. **This month**: at least 1 order read with examples, about 2,700 example
   tokens (around half a cent), and at least 1 customer look-up.

## Part 2 — The safety catch

4. Click **Turn off**. It should say it's off and applies from the next order.
5. Click **Turn on** *without* ticking the box. Expect the red box **Confirm the
   live golden check first**, and the state still **off**.
6. Tick the box, **Turn on**. It should now be **on**.
7. Tenant → **Audit →**. The top rows should be *Example prompting turned
   off* and *turned on*, each twice: once as an account event and once as a
   Console action, all by you, labelled **DocFlow support**. Tick **Also show
   Console page views** and your page visits appear too; untick to hide them.
   Scroll the rest: the tenant's whole history, from *Account created* through
   go-live and the plan changes.

## Part 3 — What the reviewer sees

What this part is for: when example prompting is on, DocFlow shows the model
up to 3 of this customer's past approved orders so it recognises their layout.
Two things must be true on screen: the reviewer is **told** examples were
used, and **no value was copied** from an old order into the new one.

8. Tenant page → **Orders →** (added 25 Sept; before that you had to add
   `/review` to the tenant page's address). The list shows **PO numbers, not
   file names**, oldest first.
9. Click the **Needs review** tab. There are two **BCH-2291** orders: open the
   one received **9/25/2026**. That's the golden order, uploaded after the
   feature was switched on. (The 9/24 one is an earlier upload of the same
   file, read without examples.) Check:
   - A grey note: **Read with 3 earlier approved orders from this customer as examples…**
   - Every value is the golden PO's: BCH-2291, 14 Mar 2026, $1,356.00, four lines ending CUP-12 at $216.00. Nothing from a seeded order (BCH-2301 to BCH-2310) appears anywhere: no other PO number, total or quantity.
10. Click the **Approved** tab and open any of **BCH-2301 to BCH-2310**, the ten
    seeded orders used as examples. They were read before the feature was on,
    so they have **no** grey note.

## Part 4 — A new order, the normal way (optional, about 3 cents)

11. As the tenant's own user (or acting-as), upload a copy of
    `docs/sample_po.txt` renamed, say, `bella-new.txt`. DocFlow will mark it a
    possible duplicate of the earlier upload, which is correct.
12. When it reaches **Needs review**, open it. The grey note should be there.
    The Console's **This month** counts should go up by one each.

## Part 5 — Switching it off really stops it

13. Turn it **off**, upload another copy (`bella-off.txt`), and open it: no grey
    note. The Console's customer look-up count doesn't move either. Off means
    no extra model call at all.

Leave it on or off as you like afterwards. All of this is test data.
