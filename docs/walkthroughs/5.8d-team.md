# Walkthrough — Slice 5.8d: the Team page

Purpose: invite a real second person through the real sign-in service, sign in
as them, then remove them and watch them get locked out. About 15 minutes. The
automated tests (8 against the real database, 7 in the browser) have passed;
this is the part only a real Supabase invite and a real second login can prove,
plus how it reads.

**Use Acme Test Prospect** (owner `ndk1516+acmetest@gmail.com`, live). You need
its admin password. For the reviewer, use another alias of your own inbox, e.g.
**`ndk1516+reviewer1@gmail.com`**. Never a real customer's address (rule 4).

## Before you start

- API, web, worker and beat are running on the new code (I restarted them).
- Migration `0023` is applied (it is).
- You'll want **two browser windows**: a normal one for the admin, and a
  **private/incognito** one for the reviewer, so the two sign-ins don't collide.
- Always use **http://localhost:3000**, not 127.0.0.1.

## Part 1 — The admin sees the Team page

1. Normal window: sign in at **http://localhost:3000/login** as the Acme Test
   Prospect admin.
2. The header now shows **Team** after Dashboard. Click it.
3. Check: one row, your address, **(you)**, **Admin**, **Signed in**, and no
   buttons on your own row.

## Part 2 — Invite a reviewer

4. Type `ndk1516+reviewer1@gmail.com` and click **Send invite**.
5. Check: a blue note says you invited them, and that DocFlow is **holding** the
   invite (no email provider is switched on yet). A second row appears:
   **Reviewer**, **Invite sent (today's date)**, with **Resend invite** and
   **Remove…** buttons.
6. Try inviting your own address again. Check: a box says **"This person is
   already on the team"**, and no row is added.
7. Try `not-an-email` (the browser may catch it first; that's fine).

## Part 3 — Accept the invite as the reviewer

8. As the founder, open **http://localhost:3000/admin/outbox** (sign out and back
   in as yourself, or use a third window). Find the newest **team_invite** email
   to `ndk1516+reviewer1@gmail.com`. Check the wording: it names who invited
   them and the company.
9. Copy the link from that email and paste it into the **private** window.
   Choose a password.
10. Check: you land on **Purchase orders** as a reviewer. The header shows
    Purchase orders, Upload, Held for review and Activity, but **no Dashboard
    and no Team**.
11. In the private window, replace the whole address with
    **http://localhost:3000/team** (not `/review/team`, which is an order
    address). Check: a box says **"This page is for account admins"**.
    Then try **http://localhost:3000/review/team**. Check: a box says **"We
    can't find that order"**.

## Part 4 — What the admin sees now

12. Back in the admin window, reload **Team**. Check: the reviewer now shows
    **Signed in**, and **Resend invite** is gone (only **Remove…** is left).
13. Open **Activity**. Check: a row saying **you invited
    ndk1516+reviewer1@gmail.com**, and an older one saying **DocFlow support
    invited** the admin (that was your original Console invite). Try the
    **Invited** filter chip.

## Part 5 — Remove the reviewer

14. On **Team**, click **Remove…** on the reviewer. Check: nothing happens yet;
    the row asks again with **Remove** / **Cancel**. Click **Cancel**, then
    **Remove…** again, then **Remove**.
15. Check: a note says they're removed and their past work stays in Activity;
    their row is gone.
16. In the **private** window (still signed in as the reviewer), click
    **Purchase orders** or reload. Check: a box says **"Your access to this
    account has ended"**, and nothing from the account is shown. Then sign out
    and sign in again as the reviewer. Check: the same message, with a **Sign
    out** button, instead of a blank "Signed in as" page.
17. Admin window, **Activity**: a **removed** row for them.

## Part 6 (optional) — Bring them back

18. On **Team**, invite `ndk1516+reviewer1@gmail.com` again. Check: the note
    says **"Welcomed back and re-invited"**. In the private window, reload.
    They're back in (their old password still works).
19. Remove them again afterwards if you'd like the account left with just you.
