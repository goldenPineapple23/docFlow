1. On the tenant page, the **Deal terms** card shows one line: what go-live
   will bill (Starter, Standard setup fee on the first invoice), with
   "Nothing is billed until go-live."
2. **Change**. Try these, saving each with **Save deal terms**:

| # | Do this | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Setup fee **Complex**, **Amount** `3000` | "That fee is outside the preset's range" (ONB-012); the range is shown under the field |
| 4 | Setup fee **Waived**, no reason | Can't save: **Why (required)** must be filled (ONB-013 if it gets through) |
| 5 | **Waived** with the reason `walkthrough test` | Saves. The line says "setup fee waived (walkthrough test)" |
| 6 | Tick **Founding customer** | Setup fee switches to **Founding**. The line under the tick box gives the founding price, for how many months, then the full price. |
| 7 | Save | The card's line now says "Starter at $…/month for N months (founding), then $… · $750 founding setup fee on the first invoice". |

Leave it on **Founding**. The **Plan** card at the top still shows the tier's
list price; the deal is what go-live bills.

## Part 3 — Step 8: Review as DocFlow support (10 min)

1. Click **Review →** on the first order.
   - **Expect:** the same review screen as the customer's, with **← Tenant**
     top left. You are acting as DocFlow support, not as their user.
   - Every line matches, CUP-12 included: this catalog has CUP-12, unlike
     Acme Test Distributor's.
2. On **04-…xlsx** (no order date, no currency), make a real correction: fill
   **Order date** `2026-03-14` and **Currency** `USD`, **Save**. Tick the
   checks, **Approve**.
3. Approve the other four, ticking their checks. The currency-missing check
   is expected: the files don't state one.
4. Back on the tenant (**← Tenant**). **Expect** "5 of 5 approved" and **Mark
   test batch complete** enabled.
5. In a private window, sign in as `uat-owner@example.test` → **Purchase
   orders** → **Approved**.
   - **Expect** the 5 orders, each flagged **Setup batch**.
   - Open 04's **History**. **Expect** your edits and approval labelled
     **DocFlow support**, never as the owner or as your email.

## Part 4 — Step 9: Go live (6 min)

1. Console: **Mark test batch complete**. **Expect** "Test batch complete." and
   the tenant header "onboarding: test batch complete".
2. **Go live** now shows the deal line. **Go live…**
   - **Expect** an amber box, **Going live will:**
     - start the Starter plan at the founding price for N months, then the
       full price; nothing billed until 7 days after go-live, when Stripe
       sends one invoice for the first month plus the $750 setup fee, due 15
       days later
     - turn on the intake address
     - send the go-live email (the invite already went in 5.1)
     - schedule the first-week check-in
   - Read it as a founder about to bill a customer. Is anything unclear?
3. **Not yet** closes it without doing anything. **Go live…** again →
   **Confirm and go live**.
   - **Expect:** "Live since …". The header says "onboarding: live". The
     intake address says "· live".
   - A **Billing** card appears: status **Free week**, **Free week ends**
     (7 days out) "— the first invoice goes out then", the setup fee, and
     **Open in Stripe ↗**.
   - **Deal terms** now says "Billed at go-live; locked." and has no Change
     link.
4. **Emails to this tenant:** **DocFlow is live for Acme Test UAT Supply**
   (held). Read it: walkthrough link and checklist.
5. **Stripe (test):** the customer now has a subscription in **Trialing**,
   ending in 7 days, a founding discount, and a pending $750 setup-fee item.
   **Open in Stripe ↗** should land on this customer.
