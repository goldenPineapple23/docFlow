# Walkthrough — Slice 5.9: billing

Purpose: one real plan change through Stripe's **test mode** (the one thing the
automated tests cannot prove, because they use a stand-in for Stripe), and a
look at the new Billing card and MRR. About 10 minutes. No real money moves.

Use **Acme Test Prospect** (live, on Growth, founding customer). Sign in as
yourself (the founder) at **http://localhost:3000/login**.

## Part 1 — The Billing card

1. Console → **Tenants** → **Acme Test Prospect**.
2. Check the **Billing** card: status **Paying**, plan **Growth (v1) — $399.00/month**
   followed by **founding price until 12/19/2026**, the setup fee line, and
   **Open in Stripe ↗**, which should open this customer in Stripe's *test*
   dashboard.

## Part 2 — Change plan, for real, in test mode

3. Click **Change plan…** and pick **Growth**. **Change plan** stays greyed
   out (they're already on it).
4. Pick **Starter**. Read the summary: from Growth to Starter, the allowance
   changes at once, Stripe prorates, the founding note, the downgrade note.
5. Click **Change plan**. Check: "Moved to Starter", with "Founding price kept
   for N more months" (N should be 2: the 19 September invoice already had the
   founding price, so 19 October and 19 November are what's left).
   The card now says Starter, still with a founding date.
6. In Stripe (test), open the customer's subscription. Check: price **$299**,
   a discount named **DocFlow founding: Starter (2 mo left)**, and a
   proration waiting for the next invoice.
7. Change the plan back to **Growth** the same way, so the account ends as it
   started. The discount in Stripe should now be the Growth one.

## Part 3 — MRR

8. Console dashboard: the **MRR** card's note should say "What paying customers
   actually pay, founding prices included". With both test tenants on founding
   prices until December, MRR should be the two founding prices added together.
