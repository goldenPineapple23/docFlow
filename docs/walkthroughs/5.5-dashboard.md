# Walkthrough — Slice 5.5: The founder dashboard

Purpose: the Console's front page should answer three questions in under ten
seconds: is everything healthy, who needs attention, and how is the business
doing. Check that each number means what its label says. About 20 minutes.
No model calls.

**Phase 5 exit criterion:** the dashboard's KPI cards match a hand-computed
query on the same data. The automated test checks every card against
hand-written SQL. Here you sanity-check the ones you can reason about.

## Before you start

- 5.3 done: Acme Test UAT Supply is live, on a free week.
- Sign in as yourself. **Attention** in the top bar is the dashboard.

---

## Part 1 — Needs your attention (3 min)

The top of the page: "Everything DocFlow has flagged for you, most severe
first. Each one was also emailed to you when it was raised."

1. **Expect:** high-severity alerts above warnings above info. Each shows its
   type in words, the tenant (a link), when it was raised, and its details.
2. Click a tenant link. **Expect** that tenant's page.
3. Judge the details line: could you act on each alert from what it says?

| 4 | **Waiting**, **Oldest waiting** | 0 and none, unless something is being read |
| 5 | **Queue (now / bulk)** | Numbers for both queues, not "—" (a dash means Redis didn't answer) |

11. **Optional:** stop the Celery worker, reload. **Expect** **Worker silent**,
    in red. Start it again (or ask me), reload: **answering**.

    "Queued — reload in a moment." After a reload, the time updates to now.
    The nightly job normally runs at 03:15 UTC.

## Part 4 — How the business is doing (6 min)

The KPI cards come from the nightly rollup, never from counting documents
while the page loads. Each shows the whole business, with a 30-day trend.

| # | Card | Sanity check |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | **Reviewed in 2 minutes** | Share approved within 120 s of first opening. Your Phase 3 timed approval should count once the rollup includes today. |
| 22 | **Received → ready to export** | Median time from arrival to approval. Seeded orders make this long; judge only that it's plausible. |
| 23 | **Approved with no edits** | The best single measure of reading quality |
| 24 | **Matched from a learned rule** | Above 0 now that Phase 2's rule has fired |
| 25 | **Corrections per 100 lines** | "Should fall month over month" |
| 26 | **Cost per document** | Mean and p95, in cents |
| 27 | **MRR** | Its note: "What paying customers actually pay, founding prices included · + $… in free trial. DocFlow's view — Stripe is the source of truth for cash". Paying: the founding prices of the live paying tenants added up (as in [5.9](5.9-billing.md)). **In free trial:** Acme Test UAT Supply's founding Starter price. |
| 28 | **Margin after AI** | (MRR − this month's AI cost) ÷ MRR: close to 100% at test volumes |
| 29 | **Retention** | 60 / 90 day. No tenant has been live 60 days yet, so expect "— / —" (not 0%), with the number of live tenants beneath |

If any card looks wrong, say which and why. I'll check it against the
hand-written SQL on the same day's data.

**Not walkable here:** the alert when the nightly figures haven't run for 36
hours, and the confidence-drift and review-backlog alerts. They need days to
pass, and the automated tests cover them.
